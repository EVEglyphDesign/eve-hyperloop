Enterprise seat — for buyers who must not be captured

The dashboard preserves the governing body's own confidence properties. The vendor never owns the memory.

EVE Glyph canon names three engagement seats. The enterprise seat is for organisations that already buy software at scale and have already paid, somewhere, for the harm that comes when a supplier owns the audit, the migration path, or the operator's institutional memory. The brief below carries two named cases: a tax-tech inversion that worked and a utility-implementation refusal that should have. Both feed the same rule: read the lock-in harm brief and acknowledge the auditor-conflict clause before vendor evaluation begins.

Lock-in harm — the thesis

Lock-in is not a price problem. It is a confidence problem.

A locked vendor relationship looks like a discount today and a hostage negotiation tomorrow. The harm is not the renewal invoice. The harm is that the governing body loses its own confidence properties — independent inspection, probability-gated escalation, evidence-backed readings, audit trails, full institutional ownership of the classified base. Those properties move into the supplier's stack and never come back.

EVE Glyph canon treats this as a welfare-register violation, not a procurement complaint. Frustration is the darkness. A system that traps the operator pushes a human toward the dark. That is harm. The two cases below show the inversion: when the seat is built on custody, the people stay free; when it is built on capture, even strong technical work gets delayed or abandoned.

Named case · 01 — the Luneau → Avalara / Vertex inversion

EVE-IC · CASE-LUN-01 · INVERSION

From a captured channel (Luneau Technology USA) to a perpetual partner (Avalara), against a closed contrast (Vertex)

Positive case for custody. Vertex held as the closed-architecture contrast that the inversion was measured against.

Background. A.I.T. Industries (the Vulich family operation in Bensenville, IL) merged into Luneau Technology USA in 2015 under a French parent; Joe Vulich led the merged US entity and the brand was later rolled into Visionix USA in 2021–22. In April 2026, the Finnish issuer Revenio announced a €290M acquisition of Visionix. The trajectory — family operator → European parent → second acquirer — is the canonical shape of channel capture: each transition narrows the operator's optionality and rewrites the institutional memory under a new owner.

The inversion. The lesson out of that shape was to refuse the same posture with tax technology. Avalara was selected as a perpetual channel partner — not because of pricing, but because the partnership model leaves the operator's confidence properties intact: try-before-you-buy at the partner tier, transparent audit posture, no vendor monopoly over the migration corridor. Custody, not capture. Vertex sits opposite on the same axis as the closed-architecture contrast — strong technology, locked engagement. The contrast is the measurement.

Captured posture · what was refused

One supplier owns the audit, the data lineage, and the renewal calendar. The buyer's confidence properties live inside the supplier's stack and re-price every cycle.

Custody posture · Avalara perpetual

Partner-tier evaluation, try-before-buy, evidence stays with the operator. Migration corridor remains open. SAP is the negative case kept on file; Avalara is the positive case kept in production.

Named case · 02 — NB Power · S/4 HANA Bayside

EVE-IC · CASE-NBP-02 · AUDITOR-CONFLICT

The legacy engagement-model supplier also served as auditor during the evaluation phase

Refusal case. The plant said no, the implementation start was delayed, and the posture was correct.

The work. The S/4 HANA project at the NB Power Bayside generating station surfaced a maintenance-data hazard at the architecture layer: equipment was being recorded as functional location rather than discrete equipment records, which would have allowed defective components to be silently recycled back into service after maintenance events. That is a human-safety register issue, not a configuration preference. The technical posture was to refuse the functional-location-only model.

The conflict. The legacy engagement-model supplier was also serving as the evaluation-phase auditor. The party that stood to win the implementation contract was the same party rendering the independent assessment. That collapses the audit trail's confidence property the moment it is set up. NB Power refused to start the implementation on those terms; the delay that followed was a feature, not a defect.

Why it stays named. NB Power supported the early EVE posture and introduced the work to the SAP Electric Utilities industry group — "the highest concentration of pier-order humans outside my family," as the founding note records. The plant must be named because the refusal is the lesson. A locked auditor is not an audit. Read the lock-in harm brief and acknowledge the auditor-conflict clause before vendor evaluation begins.

  • Hazard: functional-location maintenance posture would have allowed defective components to be recycled, masked behind a single location ID.
  • Conflict: the legacy supplier acted as evaluation-phase auditor; the audit and the contract sat in the same hand.
  • Refusal: the plant did not start the implementation on the locked-auditor model. The delay was the canon move.
  • Continuity: NB Power's posture introduced the work to the SAP Electric Utilities industry group — that introduction is the soil under the utility seat of this canon.

Try-before-you-buy · partner support model

EVE-IC · TRY-BUY-03 · AVALARA-PATTERN

Try-before-you-buy is a moral act, not a sales tactic

The pattern Avalara supports at the partner tier — and the pattern any enterprise engagement under this canon must offer.

The Avalara partner programme supports try-before-you-buy at the partner level: a working integration, in a real environment, with real evidence, before licensing is locked in. The reason this matters under EVE Glyph canon is not commercial. It is welfare-register: the operator gets to inspect their own confidence properties before they sign away the audit. If a vendor cannot afford to let the buyer see the stack run inside their estate before purchase, the vendor is asking the buyer to trade their confidence for the supplier's. That trade is the harm.

Principle · Custody, not capture

Confidence properties belong to the governing body. Any engagement model that moves independent inspection, evidence custody, or institutional memory into the supplier's stack is a canon violation regardless of price, speed, or feature parity.

Principle · No locked auditor

The party that stands to win the implementation contract cannot render the evaluation-phase audit. The NB Power refusal is the precedent. If the buyer cannot separate the audit hand from the contract hand, the buyer should not start.

Principle · Try before sign-off

If a supplier will not let the operator run the stack inside their own estate with real evidence before licensing, the supplier is asking the operator to trade confidence properties for revenue. That is the harm pattern. Avalara's partner-tier model is the working counter-example.
