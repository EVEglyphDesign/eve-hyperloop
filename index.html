About the Research

EVE is a research project on how a governing body keeps confidence in itself while it adopts sovereign-AI decision intelligence — a class of systems that interprets, classifies, and prioritizes evidence faster than any committee can.

The research observatory studies vendor-lock-in dynamics, hyperscaler commoditization, and the integration of nuclear-grade human-safety protocols into the operating practice of governing bodies. It is dashboard-shaped: each instance preserves the governing body's own confidence properties — independent inspection, probability-gated escalation, evidence-backed readings, audit trails, and full institutional ownership of the classified base accrued under its remit.

The first worked example, the EVE Hyperloop Monitor, applies the pattern today to SAP vendor-lock-in dynamics in the sovereign-AI era. The same dashboard, with classification rules adjusted, wraps any watched system the governing body chooses to extend its confidence to.
