About the Research

EVE is a research project on how a governing body keeps confidence in itself while it adopts sovereign-AI decision intelligence — a class of systems that interprets, classifies, and prioritizes evidence faster than any committee can.

The framework is dashboard-shaped, fits inside existing control-system consumption, and preserves the governing body's confidence properties: independent inspection, probability-gated escalation, evidence-backed readings, audit trails, and full institutional ownership of the classified base accrued under its remit. The first worked example applies the pattern to SAP vendor-lock-in dynamics in the sovereign-AI era.
