Canon discipline. The painted glyph is achromatic. The colour register lives in the guidance system only — surfaced on hover, in chips that float off-glyph. This separation keeps the geometry pure as a connector across substrates, and leaves the colour palette free to characterize enterprises, captures, partner ecosystems, or any other domain without ever bleeding into the canonical glyph.
Footprint cryptography — the gift. The artistic seam of the Glyph Design is this: you take a base model design and you establish ownership over your data. Everywhere you use it, the glyph leaves footprints — deliberately inconvenient ones — that cryptographically protect and tokenize your activity. Public data cannot be retroactively rewritten to change the rules of engagement after you've signed up. You don't have to say anything to the platform. Leave your footprint, and you can call them on any data that touches it. They don't own it. They don't own the interconnection anymore, and they can't leverage it. A gift back, while doing the design.
