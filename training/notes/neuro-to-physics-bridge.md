## The neuro substrate (recap)
- **Visual cortex** is *regional specialization* — V1 detects edges/orientation, V2–V4 builds shape and color, V5/MT handles motion. Two output streams: dorsal (where/how, parietal) and ventral (what, temporal).
- **Hemispheric lateralization** is *which side*, not *what kind*. Left leans language/sequential, right leans spatial/holistic — but both hemispheres co-process almost every real task via corpus callosum.
- Two orthogonal axes: **regional specialization** × **lateralization**.

## Why this matters for EVE Glyph physics
The brain is the only working precedent for a system that runs **specialized hierarchical processing** and **lateral parallel processing** in the same substrate without conflict. That's the same problem EVE Glyph solves at the protocol layer: hierarchical canon (regions) + lateral human governance (hemispheres) + corpus-callosum-equivalent arbitration.
