How to read this

  • The pyramid is one applied projection. The same four-tier shape can be spun up for any instance: delivery, AI engineering, or commercial pursuit.
  • The membrane runs through the apex tier. The apex carrier operates the membrane between the human side and the machine side. It is a working surface, not a wall.
  • The capture layer (messaging and mail inside the perimeter) means any tier or any cross-cut can be spun up as a live classified stream from inception.
  • The pyramid is a time-lapse, not a static org chart. Today's apex work becomes tomorrow's Tier 2 work as agents absorb validated patterns. The apex relocates upward to the next unsolved edge.
  • The EVE glyph sits behind the projection as its substrate.
