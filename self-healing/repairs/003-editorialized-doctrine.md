# Repair 003 — Verbatim preserve, no gloss

## Rule

The user's doctrinal input is captured **verbatim** in
`/training/writing/transcripts/` first. Only after the transcript is
saved may the agent author a derived artifact (canon-note paragraph,
protocol spec, README), and the derived artifact preserves:

- The user's word sequencing
- The user's rhythm
- French phrases as written
- Specific names, dates, modules

The agent does **not**:

- Expand named concepts with invented gloss
- Re-order the user's sequencing for "clarity"
- Insert "summary" or "in other words" recap sentences
- Substitute synonyms for the user's chosen words

## When in doubt

Quote. A pull-quote with the user's exact words beats a paraphrase
every time.

## Self-test

Diff the derived artifact against the transcript. Any word in the
artifact that is not in the transcript and not a structural connector
must justify itself. If it cannot, it is gloss. Cut it.
